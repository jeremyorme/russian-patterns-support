Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers the Learn Russian: Patterns app, labelled Russian Patterns on your device, and this support website. Jeremy Orme is responsible for the app's processing of personal data. Contact: russianpatterns.support@gmail.com.

Your choice

Usage analytics is optional. Before continuing on first use, you choose Allow or Don't allow; neither is selected for you. We rely on your consent for this analytics. Declining does not prevent you using the app.

You can withdraw consent at any time through Usage analytics preferences at the bottom of the story list. This stops new analytics collection, clears unsent buffered events and resets the app's analytics identity. A request already being sent cannot be recalled. Previously delivered events are not automatically deleted. Allowing analytics again starts a new random identity.

What optional analytics collects

When you allow analytics, we use PostHog to record:

  • A session starting.
  • A story being opened, including its story number.
  • Audio playback being requested, including the story number.
  • Practice being started, including the story number.

Events also contain timestamps, a randomly generated installation identifier, session and analytics-library information, platform and app version. The identifier lets us count distinct installations and return visits. It is pseudonymous data, not fully anonymous data; it is not your name or an advertising identifier.

We do not send your name, email address, story text, translations, selected answers, answer correctness or response times as analytics. We do not record sessions or automatically capture every tap. The app does not use analytics for advertising, cross-app tracking or sale to data brokers.

Why we use it and who receives it

We use these events to understand feature usage and whether people return, and to improve the app. PostHog processes the analytics using its EU service. Network requests expose your IP address to the service; IP-based location enrichment is disabled in our analytics configuration.

See PostHog's privacy information for information about the provider. Service providers may use subprocessors and process service-related information outside the UK or EU under their applicable data-protection arrangements.

Information on your device

The app stores your analytics choice locally. If you allow analytics, it also stores the random analytics identity and a limited queue of events awaiting delivery. Declining analytics does not upload your local learning activity.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include, through Gmail. We use this to respond and resolve your request, relying on our legitimate interest in supporting the app and its users. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Retention

Analytics events remain in PostHog until removed under the project's retention settings or deleted. We keep identifiable event records only for as long as they are needed to compare usage over time and investigate product issues; we review their continuing usefulness and remove records that are no longer needed. Support correspondence is kept while handling the request and for as long as it remains useful for resolving related issues or meeting legal obligations.

Your data rights

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw analytics consent in the app. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Email us to make a request. Analytics is associated with a random installation identifier rather than your email address, so an email address alone may not let us locate your events. We will explain what additional information, if any, is needed to find the relevant records. Please contact us before clearing app storage or withdrawing consent if you want help locating existing events, because those actions can remove the identifier.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or your local data-protection authority.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies to it. GitHub receives technical request information when serving these pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the revision date above. If a change requires fresh consent, we will request it before collecting data for that new purpose.
