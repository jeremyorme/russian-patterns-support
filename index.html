Support

Learn Russian through short stories, sentence audio, translations and explanations of language patterns.

Get in touch

For help, feedback or corrections, email russianpatterns.support@gmail.com.

For a problem with the app, include your device model, app version, what you were doing and what happened. For a story correction, include the story number and sentence. Screenshots can help; please remove personal information first.

Using the reader

Choose a story, tap a text chunk to see its translation and explanations, or use the sentence audio controls to listen. Use Practice to work with the story's exercises.

Audio problems

Check the device's media volume and whether audio is playing through connected headphones or a Bluetooth speaker. If a particular sentence fails to play, tell us its story number and sentence.

Analytics preferences

At first use, choose whether to allow optional usage analytics. You can change your choice later using Usage analytics preferences at the bottom of the story list. The app works whichever choice you make.

Read the privacy policy for details or email us with a privacy question.
